An **output** is the result produced by a [[Model]] for a given [[Input]]. It is denoted $\hat{y} = f(x)$ and belongs to an output space $\mathcal{Y}$.

**Properties**
- It is computed, not observed: a prediction or a generated result.
- Its form (class, real value, [[Probability Distribution]], structured object) depends on the [[Task]].
