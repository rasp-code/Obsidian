An **input** is the information given to a [[Model]] to produce an [[Output]]. It is denoted $x$ and belongs to an input space $\mathcal{X}$.

**Properties**
- The model is a [[Function]] $f : \mathcal{X} \to \mathcal{Y}$.
- It is numerical: a [[Vector]] or, more generally, a [[Tensor]].
- At prediction time, it is the only information the model receives about the case to process.
