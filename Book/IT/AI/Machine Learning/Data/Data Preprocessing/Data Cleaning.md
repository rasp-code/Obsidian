**Data cleaning** is the step of [[Data Preprocessing]] that detects and corrects errors and inconsistencies in the data.

**Operations**
- Missing values: removal or imputation.
- Duplicates: removal.
- Outliers: detection (see [[Anomalie Detection|Anomaly Detection]]), then removal, capping or retention.
- Inconsistencies: formats, units, invalid values.
- Label noise: correction of wrong [[Label|labels]].
