**Data reduction** decreases the volume of data while keeping the information relevant to the task.

**Operations**
- Fewer features: feature selection or [[Dimensionality Reduction]].
- Fewer examples: [[Data Sampling]].
- Fewer distinct values: discretization, aggregation.

**Purpose**
- Lower computation and storage cost, less noise and redundancy.
