**Data sampling** selects a subset of the examples of a [[Dataset]], or rebalances it.

**Principle**
- Examples are drawn at random, either uniformly, or so as to preserve class proportions (stratification), or to rebalance them (oversampling of the minority class, undersampling of the majority class).

**Condition**
- A sample is representative if it follows the same [[Probability Distribution]] as the full data.
