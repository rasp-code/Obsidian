**Underfitting** occurs when a model is too simple to capture the structure of the data.

**Symptom**
- High training error and high validation error.

**Cause**
- Insufficient capacity: high bias (see [[Bias-Variance Tradeoff]]).

**Remedies**
- More complex model, better [[Feature|features]], less [[Regularization]], longer training.
