**Evaluation** measures how well a model performs its [[Task]] on data not seen during training: the performance $P$.

**Principle**
- Performance measures are computed on a held-out set (see [[Data Splitting]]) or by [[Cross-Validation]].

**Notions**
- [[Metrics]]: the measures of performance.
- [[Generalization]]: performance on unseen data.
- [[Overfitting]], [[Underfitting]]: the two failure modes.
- [[Bias-Variance Tradeoff]]: decomposition of the error.
- [[Cross-Validation]]: estimation on several splits.
- [[Hyperparameter]], [[Hyperparameter Tuning]]: settings chosen by validation.
