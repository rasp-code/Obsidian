The **coefficient of determination** $R^2$ is the proportion of the [[Variance]] of the target explained by a [[Regression]] model.

$$R^2 = 1 - \frac{\sum_i (y_i - \hat{y}_i)^2}{\sum_i (y_i - \bar{y})^2}$$

**Properties**
- $1$: perfect prediction. $0$: as good as always predicting the mean $\bar{y}$. Negative: worse than the mean.
- Unitless.
