**Precision** is the proportion of positive predictions that are actually positive.

$$\mathrm{Precision} = \frac{TP}{TP + FP}$$

See [[Confusion Matrix]].

**Properties**
- Relevant when false positives are costly.
- Raising the decision threshold usually increases precision and decreases [[Recall]].
