**Recall** (sensitivity, true positive rate) is the proportion of actual positives that are detected.

$$\mathrm{Recall} = \frac{TP}{TP + FN}$$

See [[Confusion Matrix]].

**Properties**
- Relevant when false negatives are costly.
- Lowering the decision threshold usually increases recall and decreases [[Precision]].
