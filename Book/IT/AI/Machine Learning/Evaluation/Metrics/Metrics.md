**Metrics** are quantitative measures of the performance of a model, used for [[Evaluation]].

**Classification metrics**
- [[Confusion Matrix]]: counts of correct and wrong predictions.
- [[Accuracy]]: proportion of correct predictions.
- [[Precision]]: proportion of correct positive predictions.
- [[Recall]]: proportion of detected positives.
- [[F1-Score]]: harmonic mean of precision and recall.
- [[ROC-AUC]]: separation of the classes over all thresholds.

**Regression metrics**
- [[MAE]]: mean absolute error.
- [[MSE]]: mean squared error.
- [[R²]]: proportion of variance explained.

**Comparison**
- Unlike a [[Loss Function]], a metric need not be differentiable.
