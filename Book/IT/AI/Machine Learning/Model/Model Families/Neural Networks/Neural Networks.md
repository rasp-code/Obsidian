A model built from layers of interconnected neurons whose connections are learned from data.

## Fundamentals

- [[Neuron]]: a unit that sums its weighted inputs and fires an output signal
- [[Activation Function]]: the rule that sets how strongly a neuron fires
- [[Perceptron]]: a neuron that either fires or doesn't
- [[MLP]]: layers of neurons, each connected to the next layer
