A **model** is a parameterized [[Function]] $f_\theta : \mathcal{X} \to \mathcal{Y}$ that maps an [[Input]] to an [[Output]]; its [[Parameters]] $\theta$ are adjusted by [[Learning]].

**Notions**
- [[Parameters]]: the learned variables of a model.

**Models**
- [[Neural Network]]: composition of layers of neurons.
- [[Tree-based Models]]: successive tests on features.
- [[Naive Bayes]]: probabilistic classifier with independent features.
- [[SVM]]: maximum-margin classifier.
- [[k-NN]]: prediction from the nearest examples.
- [[k-means]]: clustering around centroids.
- [[PCA]]: projection on directions of maximal variance.
