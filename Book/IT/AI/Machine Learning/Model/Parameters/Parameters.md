The **parameters** of a [[Model]] are the internal variables, adjusted during [[Learning]], that determine the [[Function]] the model computes. They are collected in $\theta$, and the model is written $f_\theta$.

**Parameters of a neuron**
- [[Weight]]: multiplies an input.
- [[Bias]]: added to the weighted sum.

**Properties**
- They are learned from data by minimizing the [[Objective]].
- Their number measures the size of the model.

**Comparison**
- A [[Hyperparameter]] is set before learning and is not learned by the same process.
