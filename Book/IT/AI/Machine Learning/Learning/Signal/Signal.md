The **signal** is the information that tells a model how good its outputs are, and so drives [[Learning]]. Its source defines the learning setting.

**Settings**
- [[Supervised Learning]]: signal from labels.
- [[Unsupervised Learning]]: signal from the structure of the data.
- [[Self-Supervised Learning]]: signal from targets derived from the data.
- [[Reinforcement Learning]]: signal from rewards.
