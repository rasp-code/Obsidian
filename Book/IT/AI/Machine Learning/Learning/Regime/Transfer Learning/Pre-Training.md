**Pre-training** is the first phase of training a model on a large, general [[Dataset]], to learn reusable representations before adapting it to specific tasks.

**Principle**
- Usually done by [[Self-Supervised Learning]] on unlabeled data, or by [[Supervised Learning]] on a large labeled dataset.
- The resulting model is then adapted by [[Fine-Tuning]].
