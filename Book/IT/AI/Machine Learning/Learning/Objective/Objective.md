The **objective** is the function minimized during [[Learning]]: a fit term plus a complexity penalty.

$$\min_\theta \; J(\theta) + \lambda\, \Omega(\theta)$$

**Components**
- [[Fit]]: $J$, how well the model matches the training data.
- [[Regularization]]: $\lambda\, \Omega$, penalty against [[Overfitting]].
