**Structure** is the category of [[Task|tasks]] that discover structure in the data itself, without [[Label|labels]].

**Tasks**
- [[Clustering]]: groups of similar examples.
- [[Dimensionality Reduction]]: description with fewer dimensions.
- [[Density Estimation]]: the distribution of the data.
- [[Anomalie Detection|Anomaly Detection]]: examples deviating from the rest.
- [[Association Rule Learning]]: co-occurrence rules.
- [[Representation Learning]]: useful learned representations.
