**Artificial intelligence (AI)** is the field of computer science that builds systems performing tasks that normally require human intelligence: reasoning, perception, language, decision-making.

**Approaches**
- [[Symbolic AI]]: behavior from explicit rules and logic.
- [[Machine Learning]]: behavior learned from data.

**Applications**
- [[Application Domains]]: fields where AI is applied.
