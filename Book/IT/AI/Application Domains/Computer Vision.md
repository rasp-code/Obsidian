**Computer vision** is the application domain of AI that extracts information from images and videos.

**Formulation**
- [[Input]]: a [[Tensor]] of pixels (height × width × channels).
- [[Output]]: a class ([[Classification]]), a structured object such as boxes or per-pixel classes ([[Structured Prediction]]), or a new image ([[Generation]]).
