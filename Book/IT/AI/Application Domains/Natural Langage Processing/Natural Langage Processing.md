**Natural language processing (NLP)** is the application domain of AI that processes and generates human language.

**Principle**
- Text is split into **tokens** (words or sub-words), mapped to numerical [[Vector|vectors]].
- These vectors are sparse (term weights) or dense (learned embeddings, see [[Representation Learning]]).

**Methods and models**
- [[TF-IDF]]: sparse weighting of terms.
- [[LLM]]: large language model.
