**Agentic AI** refers to AI systems that pursue a goal autonomously over several steps, by planning, acting through tools, observing the results and adapting.

**Elements**
- Model: usually an [[LLM]], acting as decision maker.
- Tools: functions the agent can call to act on its environment.
- Memory: the record of previous steps.
- Loop: plan → act → observe, repeated until the goal is reached.
