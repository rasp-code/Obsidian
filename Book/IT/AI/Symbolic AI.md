**Symbolic AI** is the approach to [[AI]] in which knowledge is represented explicitly by symbols (facts, rules, logic), and intelligent behavior results from manipulating them by inference.

**Elements**
- Knowledge base: facts and rules written by humans.
- Inference engine: derives new facts by logical deduction or search.

**Comparison**
- [[Machine Learning]] infers behavior from [[Data]] instead of hand-written rules.
