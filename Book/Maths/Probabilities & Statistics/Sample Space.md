The **sample space** $\Omega$ is the set of all possible [[Outcome|Outcomes]] of a random experiment.

**Conditions**
- Collectively exhaustive: every realization of the experiment yields an outcome in $\Omega$.
- Mutually exclusive: each realization yields exactly one outcome of $\Omega$.

**Types**
- Finite: e.g. $\Omega = \{1, \dots, 6\}$.
- Countably infinite: e.g. $\Omega = \mathbb{N}$.
- Uncountable: e.g. $\Omega = \mathbb{R}$ or $\Omega = [0, 1]$.
