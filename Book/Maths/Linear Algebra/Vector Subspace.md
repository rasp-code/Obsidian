A **vector subspace** of a [[Vector Space]] $V$ is a subset $W \subseteq V$ that is itself a vector space under the operations of $V$.

**Conditions**
- $0 \in W$.
- Closed under addition: $u, v \in W \Rightarrow u + v \in W$.
- Closed under scalar multiplication: $v \in W,\ a \in \mathbb{K} \Rightarrow av \in W$.

Equivalently, $W$ is non-empty and closed under [[Linear Combination]].

**Properties**
- $\{0\}$ and $V$ are subspaces of $V$.
- The intersection of subspaces is a subspace.
- The union of two subspaces is generally not a subspace.

**Examples**
- Lines and planes through the origin in $\mathbb{R}^3$.
- The set of symmetric matrices, within the set of square matrices.
