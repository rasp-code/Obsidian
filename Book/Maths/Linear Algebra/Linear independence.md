Vectors $v_1, \dots, v_k$ are **linearly independent** if the only [[Linear Combination]] equal to the zero vector is the trivial one:

$$a_1 v_1 + \dots + a_k v_k = 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; a_1 = \dots = a_k = 0$$

They are **linearly dependent** otherwise.

**Characterization**
- The vectors are dependent if and only if at least one of them is a linear combination of the others.

**Properties**
- Any family containing the zero vector is dependent.
- Any subfamily of an independent family is independent.
- Two vectors are dependent if and only if one is a scalar multiple of the other.
- In $\mathbb{R}^n$, any family of more than $n$ vectors is dependent.
