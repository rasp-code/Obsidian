The **dimension** of a [[Vector Space]] $V$, written $\dim V$, is the number of vectors in any [[Basis]] of $V$.

**Conditions**
- $V$ has a finite basis (finite-dimensional vector space).

**Properties**
- $\dim \mathbb{R}^n = n$.
- For a [[Subspace]] $W \subseteq V$: $\dim W \leq \dim V$, with equality if and only if $W = V$.
- If $\dim V = n$:
  - any independent family has at most $n$ vectors,
  - any spanning family has at least $n$ vectors,
  - any family of $n$ vectors that is [[Linear Independence|linearly independent]] or [[Span|spanning]] is a basis.
