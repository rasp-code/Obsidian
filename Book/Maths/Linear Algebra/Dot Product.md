The **dot product** (scalar product) of two [[Vector]]s $\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{y} \in \mathbb{R}^n$ is the standard [[Inner Product]] on $\mathbb{R}^n$:

$$\mathbf{x} \cdot \mathbf{y} = \mathbf{x}^\top \mathbf{y} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i\,y_i$$

**Geometric form**
- $\mathbf{x} \cdot \mathbf{y} = \|\mathbf{x}\|\,\|\mathbf{y}\|\cos\theta$, where $\theta$ is the angle between the two vectors.

**Properties**
- Symmetric: $\mathbf{x} \cdot \mathbf{y} = \mathbf{y} \cdot \mathbf{x}$.
- Bilinear: linear in each argument.
- $\mathbf{x} \cdot \mathbf{x} = \|\mathbf{x}\|^2$ (see [[Norm]]).
- $\mathbf{x} \cdot \mathbf{y} = 0$ if and only if $\mathbf{x}$ and $\mathbf{y}$ are [[Orthogonality|orthogonal]].
