A **basis** of a [[Vector Space]] $V$ is a family of vectors that is both:
- [[Linear Independence|linearly independent]],
- spanning $V$ (see [[Span]]).

**Properties**
- Every vector $v \in V$ is written in a unique way as a [[Linear Combination]] of the basis vectors. The coefficients are the **coordinates** of $v$ in that basis.
- All bases of $V$ have the same number of vectors.
- A basis is a minimal spanning family and a maximal independent family.

**Standard basis of $\mathbb{R}^n$**
- $e_i$ has a $1$ at position $i$ and $0$ elsewhere.
