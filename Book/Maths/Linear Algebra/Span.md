The **span** of vectors $v_1, \dots, v_k$ of a [[Vector Space]] is the set of all their [[Linear Combination|linear combinations]]:

$$\mathrm{Span}(v_1, \dots, v_k) = \left\{ \sum_{i=1}^{k} a_i v_i \;\middle|\; a_i \in \mathbb{K} \right\}$$

**Properties**
- It is a [[Vector Subspace]].
- It is the smallest vector subspace containing $v_1, \dots, v_k$.
- Adding to the family a vector already in its span does not change the span.
- $\mathrm{Span}(\emptyset) = \{0\}$.

**Generating family**
- The family $(v_1, \dots, v_k)$ **spans** (or generates) $V$ if $\mathrm{Span}(v_1, \dots, v_k) = V$.
