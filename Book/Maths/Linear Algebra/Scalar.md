A **scalar** is a single element of the field $\mathbb{K}$ over which a [[Vector Space]] is defined (usually $\mathbb{R}$). It is a quantity fully described by one number, with no direction or other structure.

**Examples**
- Real numbers: $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}$.
- Complex numbers: $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{C}$.

**Tensor view**
- A scalar is a tensor of order $0$ (shape $()$).
